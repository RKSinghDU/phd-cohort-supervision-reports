1.4 Resolving C6 Dilemmas — Specific Guidance

  1. CGHRM lacks established scales: Conceptual paper + scale-development paper first; empirical chapter follows. Reading: MacKenzie, Podsakoff & Podsakoff (2011); Hinkin (1998); Pham et al. (2023) Appendix A.
  2. DV choice (social sustainability / well-being / pro-social behaviour): Most foundational unresolved question. Recommend OCB-Society / employee pro-social behaviour as primary DV.
  3. Ethical culture vs ethical climate as IV: Theory-driven choice required. CGHRM is rooted in values; ethical culture is theoretically correct. Reading: Treviño, Butterfield & McCabe (1998); Treviño & Weaver (2003).
  4. Search string: Sample — TITLE-ABS-KEY(("common good HRM" OR ("sustainable HRM" AND ("common good" OR dignity OR humanistic))) AND (ethic* OR "ethical culture" OR "ethical leadership")) AND PUBYEAR > 2018
  5. Theories underpinning the model: EC → CGHRM: Schein / Treviño culture frameworks. EL moderator: Social Learning Theory (Bandura 1977; Brown, Treviño & Harrison 2005). CGHRM → behaviour: AMO theory or Social Exchange. Umbrella: Stakeholder Theory (Freeman 1984).

2.4 Diagnostic Frame

A four-month silence after a promising start usually has one of four causes:

  1. Personal / health / family circumstances she has not disclosed
  2. Loss of confidence — the topic now feels too large
  3. Loss of direction — she doesn't know what step comes next
  4. Loss of motivation — competing priorities or doubts about the PhD

The next supervision meeting is NOT a methodological correction. It is a wellbeing-and-commitment check. Establish first whether she is still in this, what is blocking her, and what one small concrete step she can complete in the next 7 days.
